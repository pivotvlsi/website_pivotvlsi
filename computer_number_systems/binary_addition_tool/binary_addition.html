Frequently Asked Questions: Binary Addition

How do I add two binary numbers?

This tool teaches you the step-by-step process of adding two binary numbers using the Full Adder logic. First, you need to convert the given decimal numbers to binary. Then, fill out the Truth table. Finally, add the numbers bit by bit starting from the right (LSB).

What is a Full Adder?

A full adder adds binary numbers and accounts for values carried in as well as out. It has three inputs (A, B, and Carry-in) and two outputs (Sum and Carry-out).
