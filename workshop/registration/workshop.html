5-Session Curriculum: Jane Street Protocol

We will cover the entire Jane Street protocol in 5 comprehensive sessions. In our very first session, we will cover the CPU architecture in depth:

  • Session 1: CPU Architecture & ISA: We will cover the CPU architecture — register files, custom ISA, datapath execution, instruction decoder, ALU operations, and hardware simulator walkthrough.
  • Session 2: Protocol Deep-Dive & Framing: In-depth breakdown of the Jane Street market data/order protocol specifications, binary packet framing, state machines, and header structures.
  • Session 3: Ultra-Low Latency Hardware Design: Pipeline design, hardware acceleration, minimal cycle-latency trade-offs, and clock-domain crossing (CDC).
  • Session 4: RTL Implementation & Simulation: Translating algorithmic protocol logic into cycle-accurate synthesizable Verilog/SystemVerilog and robust testbenches.
  • Session 5: System Verification & Interview Strategy: End-to-end hardware-software co-verification, past interview problem patterns, and candidate preparation roadmap.

Who Should Attend

Students (undergraduate & graduate) and aspiring engineers actively preparing to crack VLSI and semiconductor interviews, as well as digital design and FPGA developers aiming for top-tier hardware and systems engineering roles.
