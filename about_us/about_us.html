About PIVOT

Making VLSI education practical — from learning digital design to building real silicon. PIVOT is focused on bridging the gap between academic VLSI education and real semiconductor engineering through interactive learning, expert-led lectures, and hands-on silicon design experiences. We are proudly pioneering the effort to bring TinyTapeout to India, as SaralTapeout enabling students and professionals to design, tape out, and manufacture their own custom silicon chips. Currently we are in process of market study in India as tapeout involves huge cost.

To further our commitment to excellence in education, PIVOT is continuously expanding its collection of interactive learning tools and codelabs to deeply enhance the practical understanding of our students.

Our Mission

To democratize VLSI education by providing accessible, premier educational programs, interactive codelabs, and expert video lectures that bridge the gap between academic learning and industry requirements.

Our Vision

To become India's leading platform for semiconductor innovation, empowering the next generation of engineers to turn their silicon dreams into reality and fuel the global VLSI ecosystem.

What is SaralTapeout?

SaralTapeout makes it possible for designers, students, and educators to submit small digital designs for fabrication on a shared silicon chip. It provides an accessible way to experience the real ASIC journey.
